01 /

ORDER PROCESSING

Once an order is placed, its details may need to be reviewed and confirmed before preparation begins. Processing includes checking item availability and preparing the order for dispatch.

Processing time may vary during busy periods, product launches, public holidays, or when additional order verification is required.

GOOD TO KNOW ↗

Order processing and delivery transit are separate stages. A delivery estimate should not be treated as confirmed until it has been communicated for your order.

02 /

SHIPPING LOCATIONS

Shipping availability depends on the destination and the delivery services available for that area.

If you are unsure whether delivery is available to your location, please contact our team before placing your order.

ASK ABOUT YOUR LOCATION ↗
03 /

DELIVERY ESTIMATES

Estimated delivery times can vary depending on the destination, courier service, order processing requirements, and external conditions.

Where an estimate is provided, it is intended as a guide rather than a guaranteed arrival date unless expressly confirmed otherwise.

DELIVERY TIMEFRAMES ↗

Specific delivery timeframes have not yet been published. Please contact VOID District for the latest information about your destination.

04 /

SHIPPING CHARGES

Shipping charges, if applicable, depend on the destination and delivery arrangements. Any applicable charges should be communicated before an order is finalized.

Please review the final order amount and delivery details before confirming your purchase.

05 /

TRACKING YOUR ORDER

If a tracking service is available for your shipment, tracking details may be provided once the parcel has been handed over to the delivery provider.

Tracking updates depend on the courier and may not appear immediately after dispatch.

NEED AN UPDATE? ↗

Contact our support team with your order reference if you need assistance checking the status of your shipment.

06 /

DELAYS & DISRUPTIONS

Occasionally, deliveries may be affected by weather, courier operations, public holidays, address issues, or other circumstances outside normal control.

If your order appears delayed, please reach out to our support team so the available shipment information can be reviewed.

07 /

ADDRESS INFORMATION

Please provide an accurate and complete delivery address, along with a reachable phone number and any relevant delivery instructions.

Incorrect or incomplete address details can lead to delivery delays or unsuccessful delivery attempts. If you notice an error, contact us as soon as possible.

IMPORTANT ↗

Address changes may not be possible once an order has been dispatched.

08 /

SHIPPING SUPPORT

Questions about delivery availability, shipping charges, tracking, or a delayed parcel? We're here to help you find the right information.

When contacting support about an existing order, include your order reference and a brief description of your concern. Do not share sensitive payment information.
