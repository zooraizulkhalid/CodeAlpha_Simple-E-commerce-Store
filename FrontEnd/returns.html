01 /

REQUEST A RETURN

If you'd like to request a return, start by contacting VOID District customer support. Include your order reference, the product involved, and a short explanation of your request.

Our team can review the information and advise you about the available next steps. Please wait for instructions before sending an item back.

BEFORE YOU CONTACT US ↗

Have your order reference and product details ready. For a product issue, photographs may help our team understand the concern.

CONTACT CUSTOMER SUPPORT ↗
02 /

RETURN ELIGIBILITY

Return eligibility may depend on the product, its condition, the order details, and the applicable return conditions.

Please contact our team to confirm whether your item is eligible before arranging a return. Any applicable request deadline should also be confirmed with support.

RETURN WINDOW ↗

A specific return request period has not yet been published. Please confirm the applicable timeframe with VOID District before requesting a return.

03 /

PRODUCT EXCHANGES

Need a different size or want to ask about another product? Contact our support team with your order details and the exchange you'd like to request.

Exchange options may depend on product availability, item condition, and the applicable store conditions. An exchange is not confirmed until our team has reviewed and approved the request.

SIZE & AVAILABILITY ↗

Product sizes and stock levels can change. Please confirm the availability of your preferred replacement before making any return arrangements.

04 /

ITEM CONDITION

The condition of a product may affect whether a return or exchange can be accepted. When submitting a request, please describe the item's current condition accurately.

Keep any original packaging, labels, tags, and accompanying items available until you receive further instructions from support.

HELPFUL REMINDER ↗

Avoid sending products back without authorization. The team may need to review your request before providing the correct return instructions.

05 /

DAMAGED OR INCORRECT ITEMS

If an order arrives damaged, appears defective, or contains an incorrect item, please contact VOID District support with the relevant order details.

Explain the issue clearly and, where possible, include photographs of the product and packaging. Our team can review the concern and advise you about the available resolution options.

REPORT AN ORDER ISSUE ↗
06 /

RETURN SHIPPING

Return shipping arrangements depend on the nature of the request and the instructions provided after review.

Before sending an item, confirm the correct return address, packaging requirements, available delivery method, and any applicable shipping charges with our support team.

SHIPPING COSTS ↗

Responsibility for return shipping charges has not yet been published as a general policy. Please confirm any applicable costs before dispatching a return.

VIEW SHIPPING POLICY ↗
07 /

REFUND INFORMATION

Returns and refunds are related but separate processes. Approval of a return request does not automatically confirm a refund.

Any refund eligibility, amount, payment method, and processing arrangements are subject to the applicable refund conditions and review.

REFUND DETAILS ↗

For information about refund eligibility and processing, refer to our Refund Policy or contact customer support.

VIEW REFUND POLICY ↗
08 /

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Still have questions about returns, exchanges, product condition, or a previous order? Our support team can help you understand the available options.

When contacting us, include your order reference and a brief description of your concern. Please do not share passwords or sensitive payment information.
