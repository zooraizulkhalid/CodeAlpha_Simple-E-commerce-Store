01 /

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Depending on how you interact with VOID District, we may collect personal information that you provide directly, such as your name, email address, telephone number, billing or delivery address, and information included in support requests.

When you place an order, we may process details relating to the items purchased, delivery, transaction status, and order history. If customer accounts are available, we may also process account credentials and account preferences.

Our website and service providers may also collect technical information such as device type, browser information, IP address, and website usage data, depending on the technologies enabled.

DATA MINIMIZATION ↗

We aim to collect information relevant to providing our services and operating our business, subject to applicable privacy laws.

02 /

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

Personal information may be used to process purchases, arrange delivery, communicate order updates, respond to customer inquiries, manage refunds, and provide customer support.

Where customer accounts are available, information may also be used to support authentication, saved addresses, account settings, and order history.

We may also use relevant information to maintain website functionality, prevent fraud, meet legal obligations, and improve our services, where permitted by law.

LAWFUL PROCESSING ↗

Where required, VOID District will rely on an appropriate legal basis for processing personal information, such as fulfilling a contract, complying with legal duties, legitimate interests where permitted, or obtaining consent.

03 /

CUSTOMER ACCOUNTS & GUEST CHECKOUT

VOID District plans to offer both guest checkout and optional customer accounts. Customers using guest checkout will not need to create an account solely to complete a purchase.

Registered customers will be able to use account features as they become available, which may include viewing orders, maintaining profile details, and saving delivery addresses.

Account information is intended to be handled through our planned Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB backend. These technologies are part of our implementation plan and do not, by themselves, establish that particular security controls are already operational.

ACCOUNT SECURITY ↗

Customers should use strong, unique passwords and avoid sharing login credentials. Authentication and account security measures must be verified before account registration is launched.

04 /

PAYMENTS & TRANSACTION DATA

When payment functionality is available, payment information may be processed by the payment providers selected for VOID District checkout.

We may receive transaction details needed to confirm purchases, manage orders, investigate payment issues, and process approved refunds.

The information received and retained will depend on the payment integration used. Payment providers may also process data under their own privacy notices.

PAYMENT INTEGRATION ↗

The payment provider and final checkout configuration have not yet been confirmed. Their names, data practices, and any international transfers must be disclosed before live payments are accepted.

05 /

COOKIES & TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

VOID District may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to support website functionality, including shopping cart preferences and other user-selected settings.

Analytics, advertising, and other optional tracking technologies may be introduced as the website develops. Where applicable law requires consent, such technologies will need appropriate consent controls before activation.

Browser settings may allow customers to manage certain cookies or stored website information. Disabling necessary storage may affect shopping functionality.

COOKIE TRANSPARENCY ↗

A final inventory of cookies, local storage, analytics services, and other trackers must be completed before this notice is published for live customers.

06 /

MARKETING EMAILS & CONSENT

VOID District intends to send promotional emails, product announcements, and special offers only to customers who have provided the required marketing consent.

Marketing consent will be optional and separate from the requirement to complete a purchase. Customers will be able to withdraw consent and unsubscribe from promotional communications.

Order confirmations, payment receipts, shipping updates, and important service-related communications may still be sent where necessary for fulfilling orders or meeting legal obligations.

YOUR CHOICE ↗

Unsubscribing from marketing messages will not prevent essential communications about an active order.

07 /

DATA SHARING & SECURITY

Providing e-commerce services may require sharing relevant information with selected service providers, such as payment processors, delivery companies, hosting providers, email platforms, and technical support services.

Information may also need to be disclosed when required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

VOID District intends to implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards to reduce risks of unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure.

BEFORE LAUNCH ↗

Security measures, service-provider arrangements, and actual data-sharing practices must be reviewed and documented before customer data collection begins.

08 /

INTERNATIONAL CUSTOMERS & PRIVACY RIGHTS

VOID District plans to serve customers in Pakistan and other countries. Depending on where a customer lives and which laws apply, they may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction, portability, or withdrawal of consent.

Customer information may be processed or stored in countries other than the customer's own, depending on the hosting and service providers used. Where required, appropriate safeguards for international data transfers must be implemented.

Requests to exercise privacy rights will be assessed according to applicable law, including any identity verification and lawful exceptions.

REGIONAL REQUIREMENTS ↗

Privacy requirements can differ by country. This policy does not remove or restrict rights provided under applicable data protection laws.

09 /

DATA RETENTION & DELETION

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is processed, including order fulfillment, customer support, legitimate business needs, and applicable legal obligations.

Certain records may need to be retained for accounting, taxation, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, or other lawful purposes.

Customers may request deletion of eligible personal information, subject to applicable legal exceptions and retention requirements.

RETENTION SCHEDULE ↗

Specific retention periods will need to be established for order records, accounts, support messages, marketing preferences, and technical logs before the live service launches.

10 /

CONTACT & PRIVACY REQUESTS

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to submit a request concerning your personal information, please contact VOID District through our Contact Us page.

Include enough information to help us identify and review your request, but do not submit passwords, payment card security codes, or other unnecessary sensitive details.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on certain requests. Responses will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements.
