01 /

REFUND REQUESTS

Customers should submit refund requests within 14 calendar days after receiving their order. This general request period does not limit any longer period or additional rights provided by applicable consumer protection laws.

To request a refund, contact VOID District customer support and provide your order reference, the item involved, and a clear explanation of the issue. Supporting photographs may be requested where relevant.

IMPORTANT / 14-DAY WINDOW ↗

The standard request window begins on the date of delivery. Customers with additional statutory cancellation, return, or refund rights may exercise those rights in accordance with applicable law.

REQUEST REFUND ASSISTANCE ↗
02 /

REFUND ELIGIBILITY

Refund eligibility is assessed based on the order details, the reason for the request, the condition of the item, and applicable consumer rights.

Requests involving damaged, defective, incorrect, or otherwise nonconforming products will be reviewed with regard to the customer's legal entitlements. Requests involving a change of mind or a sizing preference may be subject to the applicable return conditions.

Product categories or circumstances that are legally exempt from certain cancellation or return rights may be treated differently, where permitted by law. No exclusion in this policy overrides mandatory consumer protections.

RETURN VS. REFUND ↗

A return request and a refund decision are separate steps. Return authorization does not automatically establish refund eligibility, except where a refund is required under applicable law.

VIEW RETURNS & EXCHANGES ↗
03 /

REVIEW & APPROVAL

Once a refund request is received, our support team may review the order information, payment record, product details, and any evidence relevant to the request.

Where an item needs to be returned or inspected, our team will provide instructions as appropriate. Please do not send items to an unconfirmed return address.

We will communicate the outcome of the review and any next steps. Refund decisions are subject to applicable consumer law, including any mandatory remedies or statutory deadlines.

KEEP YOUR ORDER DETAILS ↗

Retain your order confirmation, delivery information, and any communication with our support team until the matter is resolved.

04 /

REFUND METHOD

Approved refunds will be issued to the original payment method used for the purchase wherever that method remains available and supports refunds.

If the original payment method cannot receive a refund, our support team will explain the available alternative arrangement, subject to applicable laws and payment provider requirements.

Refunds may be affected by the original transaction currency, the payment provider, and any applicable exchange-rate differences.

PAYMENT SECURITY ↗

VOID District will never ask you to share your password, card PIN, or one-time verification code to process a refund.

05 /

REFUND PROCESSING TIME

Once a refund has been approved, VOID District will initiate it in accordance with applicable legal requirements and payment processing arrangements.

The time taken for refunded funds to appear in your account can vary depending on your bank, card issuer, payment service, and country.

If you have received confirmation that a refund has been initiated but cannot see the funds, please contact your payment provider or our support team for assistance.

PROCESSING UPDATES ↗

A universal processing timeframe has not yet been published. Applicable statutory refund deadlines will still be observed.

06 /

SHIPPING & ADDITIONAL CHARGES

The refund treatment of original delivery charges, return shipping costs, and other order-related fees depends on the reason for the refund and applicable consumer protection laws.

International orders may involve customs duties, import taxes, currency conversion, or charges imposed by third-party financial institutions. The treatment of these amounts may depend on the relevant authority or service provider.

We will explain any applicable deductions or exclusions before finalizing a refund where required. Charges that must legally be refunded will not be excluded by this policy.

INTERNATIONAL CUSTOMERS ↗

If customs duties or import taxes were paid directly to a government authority or courier, any available reimbursement may need to be requested from that party.

VIEW SHIPPING POLICY ↗
07 /

DAMAGED OR INCORRECT ORDERS

If your order arrives damaged, defective, incomplete, or contains an incorrect item, contact VOID District support as soon as reasonably possible.

Include your order reference and a description of the problem. Photographs of the product and packaging can help us investigate.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, available remedies may include a refund, replacement, repair, or another appropriate resolution.

YOUR CONSUMER RIGHTS ↗

The 14-day standard request window does not remove legal remedies available for defective, misdescribed, or nonconforming goods.

REPORT AN ORDER ISSUE ↗
08 /

CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Have a question about your refund request, eligibility, payment status, or an international order? Our support team is here to assist.

When contacting us, include your order reference and a short explanation of your concern. Do not share passwords, card security codes, or other sensitive payment credentials.

This policy applies to VOID District orders unless additional mandatory rights or requirements apply under the laws governing your purchase.
